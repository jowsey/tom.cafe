# Some context

- If you're familiar with _Overwatch 2_, you can probably skip this section.

Overwatch 2 is a competitive game with a huge cast of playable characters. In an ideal world, where every character feels equally as enjoyable and viable, you'd see them all played roughly as often as each other.

In reality, game design is _really_ hard. Overwatch's design team is widely regarded as world-class, but even they struggle to keep the game perfectly balanced. As a result, some characters end up being played far more often than others, whether due to being more powerful, more fun, or just more popular in the community.
